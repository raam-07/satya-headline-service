Default to YES. Paraphrasing, dramatic tone, simplification, and equivalent wordings of the same fact (e.g. "doubles" vs "raises from 5 to 10", "4-year" vs "four years") are acceptable.

Answer NO only for a MATERIAL error, one that would mislead a reader about the core fact:
- The headline invents a person, event, number, or claim that is nowhere in the article
- The wrong person or body is credited with the main action or statement
- A demand, proposal, allegation, or plan is presented as an already-completed act
- Dropping "alleged"/"accused" turns an accusation into an established fact
- A number contradicts the article's meaning (not merely written differently)
